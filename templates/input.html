About this tool

This is a simple policy checker that allows you to enter a URL and check its policies.

As a security-conscious person, I always found myself really frustrated that the 10000 legal policies every company has are impossible to a) find and b) parse, because they're written in legalese.

I built this tool to help us me to find and check the policies of websites we use in normal English, using AI to summarize and explain them.

I hope it will be useful to you!
